Privacy Policy — Jay C

Last updated: November 2026

Scope

Jay C is a private, single-owner personal assistant running locally on the owner's Mac. It is not distributed or made available to third-party users.

Data Accessed & How It Is Used

When authorized by the account owner via Google OAuth, Jay C accesses Google Workspace data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts) solely to fulfill direct requests initiated by the account owner.

Storage, Sharing & Deletion

OAuth credentials are stored locally in an encrypted credential file on the owner's device. Google user data is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising. Prompt context may be processed by the AI model provider selected by the owner solely to generate requested responses. Access may be revoked at any time from Google Account Permissions, and local credentials can be deleted at any time via gws auth logout.
